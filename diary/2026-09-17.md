## Notes
Over the previous 2 days I finalised and submitted my project proposal, which is included in this repository as `proposal.md`.

To prepare for this, over the last week I watched the ~20 minute online discussion around the project from Moodle, the Spotify podcast, information slides, as well as skimmed the 25/26 manual (time constrained as this was unavailable to me and isn't available *anywhere* online) and some other proposal submissions.

To sum up what I've learned from the current FYP resources on Moodle [unordered]:
- While the project is important, being double-weighted in Y4S2 to 10 credits, don't neglect other modules.
- The project process and documentation make-up a large proportion of the final result. Use the project management tool that makes sense; choose your favourite: Miro/Trello/etc. and don't feel the need to go overboard (like using two). Kanban is probably most appropriate, but doesn't look as good as Scrum with its burndown and velocity charts.
- Get something working early, ideally by February, to have as a fall-back.
- Use a VCS and CI/CD workflow.
- Set a firm end-date for development. Important to get in the right mindset for the final report.
- Develop a list of features around an MVP and work towards this exclusively at first.
- SSD students are expected to do software projects, not research. Plus, the research projects are a significant undertaking as the expectation is for publish-standard quality.
- Emphasis on system design, models, architectures, etc. Not so much on implementation.
